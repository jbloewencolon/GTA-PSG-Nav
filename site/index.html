      How this count works

      The guardrails rise with what you described, not with how many rules apply. Each of these adds posts once: personal or sensitive information (2), a decision about a person (2), something the public sees or interacts with (1), buying or building an AI tool (1), AI content in an official document (1), legal analysis or AI note-taking in meetings (1). A Not sure answer counts as yes. 0 posts is Baseline rules only, 1–2 Light, 3–4 Standard, 5 or more Extra. These values are this tool’s, not GTA’s, and the count is not a GTA rating. A red stop sign appears when the documents require GTA approval for something you described, or may prohibit it. An amber Check first sign appears when they require a review or authorization first.
